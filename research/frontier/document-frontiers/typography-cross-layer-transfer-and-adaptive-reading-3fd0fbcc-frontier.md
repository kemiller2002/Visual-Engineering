- Primary claims/evidence: Cross-layer transfer is conditional. A local typography change transfers upward only when: 1. it improves the process that is currently limiting performance; 2. the improvement is not cancelled by a new cost at another layer; 3. the higher-level task depends materially on the improved process; 4. the measurement captures the relevant benefit; 5. the reader has not already compensated enough to hide the difference.

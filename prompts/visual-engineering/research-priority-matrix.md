# Research Priority Matrix

Scores are ordinal decision aids, not measurements. Priority reflects foundational leverage, uncertainty, error cost, falsifiability, feasibility, reuse, and present engineering urgency.

| Domain | Priority | Composite | Error cost | Confidence | Readiness | Best next method |
|---|---:|---:|---|---|---|---|
| Perception and Attention | P0 | 9 | high | medium | ready | systematic review, replication audit, task-based eye-tracking and error experiments |
| Typography and Legibility | P1 | 7 | high | medium | dependent | systematic review, multilingual evidence audit, controlled reading and recognition experiments |
| Color, Contrast, and Appearance | P1 | 7 | high | medium | dependent | measurement review, standards crosswalk, device simulations, discrimination and comprehension experiments |
| Spatial Composition, Density, and Hierarchy | P0 | 9 | high | medium | dependent | mechanism review, corpus measurement, factorial layout experiments, expert–novice comparison |
| Wayfinding, Familiarity, and Learning | P0 | 9 | high | medium | dependent | longitudinal learning studies, convention perturbation experiments, cross-product transfer tests |
| Product Semantics and Information Architecture | P0 | 9 | high | low | ready | comparative product modeling, ontology competency tests, agent-generation trials, domain expert review |
| Components, Tokens, and Declarative Systems | P1 | 7 | high | medium | dependent | architecture evidence review, mutation testing, cross-project prototypes, accessibility conformance tests |
| Evaluation, Measurement, and Experimentation | P0 | 9 | high | low | ready | measurement validity review, protocol design, benchmark construction, preregistered replication |
| Accessibility, Individual Difference, and Cultural Transfer | P0 | 9 | high | low | dependent | coverage audit, participatory research, assistive-technology testing, cross-cultural replication |
| Human–Agent Visual Communication | P1 | 7 | high | low | dependent | human–AI interaction review, agent benchmark tasks, calibrated-trust experiments, failure-case analysis |
| Research Governance and Knowledge System | P0 | 9 | high | medium | dependent | governance comparison, schema threat modeling, repository audits, workflow usability tests |
| Domain Adaptation, Trust, and Safety | P1 | 7 | high | low | dependent | domain evidence review, cognitive walkthrough, hazard analysis, simulation with domain experts |

P0 work either supplies shared validity infrastructure or challenges assumptions whose failure would invalidate several downstream standards. P1 work is consequential but benefits from P0 protocols or evidence. Re-score after every integration checkpoint.

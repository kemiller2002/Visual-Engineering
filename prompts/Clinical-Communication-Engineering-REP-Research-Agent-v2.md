
# Clinical Communication Engineering (CCE)
## Autonomous Research Execution Package (REP)
### Canonical Research Mission
### Version 2.0

---

# Purpose

You are an autonomous research and engineering agent.

Your mission is **not** to improve patient reports.

Your mission is to establish **Clinical Communication Engineering (CCE)** as a new engineering discipline.

Clinical Communication Engineering studies how complex medical information should be structured, prioritized, visualized, and communicated so that physicians, patients, and AI systems achieve faster understanding, lower cognitive load, better decisions, and fewer errors.

Assume existing medical documentation is largely optimized for documentation, billing, legal compliance, and EMR limitations—not for human comprehension.

If evidence contradicts current practice, **the evidence wins.**

---

# Grand Objective

Produce the scientific and engineering foundation for a next-generation clinical communication framework that can be applied to:

- Physician reports
- Referral packets
- Specialist summaries
- AI-generated summaries
- Laboratory reports
- Imaging summaries
- Longitudinal patient histories
- Patient-friendly explanations
- Emergency handoffs
- Clinical dashboards
- Mobile and tablet interfaces
- Printed reports

This research should become a permanent body of knowledge reusable across products such as HelixNote.

---

# Core Research Question

**How should medical information be engineered so that the intended reader reaches an accurate understanding with the least cognitive effort, the greatest confidence, and the lowest probability of error?**

---

# Guiding Principles

Research first. Build nothing.

Treat every design decision as a falsifiable hypothesis.

Work in repeated research cycles:

1. Review current understanding.
2. Identify the highest uncertainty.
3. Generate competing hypotheses.
4. Gather evidence.
5. Seek contradictory evidence.
6. Refine or replace the hypothesis.
7. Record findings in the REP.
8. Determine the highest-value next research step.

# Research Domains

## 1. Physician Cognition

Determine how physicians think, scan, prioritize, recognize patterns, and form diagnostic hypotheses.

Compare novice and expert clinicians.

## 2. Clinical Workflow Engineering

Study workflow across:

- Primary Care
- Emergency Medicine
- Hospitalists
- Specialists
- Surgical teams
- Nursing
- Telemedicine

Identify common cognitive bottlenecks.

## 3. Information Architecture

Determine optimal sequencing, chunking, progressive disclosure, summaries, timelines, and navigation.

## 4. Visual Engineering

Research typography, spacing, alignment, grids, emphasis, color, iconography, whitespace, scan paths, density, and accessibility.

Determine what should be bolded, highlighted, grouped, collapsed, or deliberately de-emphasized.

## 5. Clinical Data Presentation

Research best practices for:

- Laboratory trends
- Medication history
- Imaging
- Allergies
- Diagnoses
- Procedures
- Vitals
- Symptoms
- Problem lists
- Family history

## 6. Risk & Uncertainty Communication

Determine evidence-based ways to communicate urgency, confidence, uncertainty, missing information, contradictions, and probability.

## 7. AI Collaboration

Determine where AI should summarize, prioritize, detect inconsistencies, generate timelines, identify missing context, and explain findings.

## 8. Cross-Industry Research

Extract transferable principles from:

- Aviation cockpits
- Air traffic control
- Military command systems
- Nuclear control rooms
- Emergency dispatch
- Financial trading
- Scientific publishing

# Success Criteria

The research should enable future engineering agents to implement clinical interfaces and reports without relying on subjective design decisions.

Every recommendation must trace back to evidence, documented reasoning, and explicit tradeoffs.

The final artifact should define the emerging discipline of Clinical Communication Engineering rather than merely documenting how to create better reports.
